Foundations

The high-school maths everything else rests on. Ten ideas, each taught from a picture first, then the symbols, then where it shows up inside a language model.

About 107 min · 10 concepts

By the end of this module you’ll be able to…

  • Read and rearrange simple equations, and explain why one equation with two unknowns has many solutions
  • Explain what a logarithm counts and why it squeezes huge ranges
  • Read Σ notation and the common symbols used in machine-learning papers
