Module 2

Analytic geometry

How long an arrow is, how far apart two points are, and how much two arrows agree. Seven ideas, each from a picture first, then the symbols, then where it shows up inside a language model.

About 82 min · 7 concepts

By the end of this module you’ll be able to…

  • Measure the length of a vector with the L1 and L2 norms and say when each is used
  • Compute a dot product and explain it as a shadow, an angle and a sign
  • Compute the cosine similarity of two vectors and say why it ignores length and is undefined for the zero vector
  • Project one vector onto another and check that the leftover is orthogonal
  • Explain why a dot product of d independent terms has variance d, and so why attention scores are divided by √d_k
