Linear algebra

Lists of numbers that can also be arrows, and tables of numbers that act as machines. Four ideas, each from a picture first, then the symbols, then where it shows up inside a language model.

Systems of equations, span and linear transformations come in the next part.

About 58 min · 4 concepts

By the end of this module you’ll be able to…

  • Multiply a matrix by a vector and explain what it does to space
  • Describe a vector both as an arrow and as a list of numbers
  • Build new vectors from linear combinations of old ones
