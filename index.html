The maths behind machine learning

Learn it alone, then explain it to someone else. Each idea starts from a picture, adds the notation, and ends with where it shows up in a large language model.

Start the course

What you’ll learn

  • Read and rearrange simple equations, and explain why one equation with two unknowns has many solutions
  • Multiply a matrix by a vector and explain what it does to space

Who it’s for

You need high-school maths and curiosity. Nothing is too simple to explain, and nothing is locked: suggestions about what to read first are signposts, never gates.

How this course works

  1. 7 stages per idea. Meaning, picture, real life, a worked example, why and when, how to arrive at it, and where it appears in a language model.
  2. Hover or tap any underlined term for a plain-language and a mathematical definition.
  3. Mark complete when you could teach it. Progress stays in this browser only, so you can carry it between devices with the file below.
