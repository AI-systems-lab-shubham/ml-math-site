What you’ll learn

  • Read and rearrange simple equations, and explain why one equation with two unknowns has many solutions
  • Explain what a logarithm counts and why it squeezes huge ranges
  • Read Σ notation and the common symbols used in machine-learning papers
  • Multiply a matrix by a vector and explain what it does to space
More outcomes (2)
  • Describe a vector both as an arrow and as a list of numbers
  • Build new vectors from linear combinations of old ones

Who it’s for

You need high-school maths and curiosity. Nothing is too simple to explain, and nothing is locked: suggestions about what to read first are signposts, never gates.
